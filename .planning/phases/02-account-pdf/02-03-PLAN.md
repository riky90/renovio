## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-01 | Elevation of Privilege | brute-force login | mitigate | `@limiter.limit("5/minute")` su /login e /registrati (slowapi condiviso) |
| T-03-02 | Information Disclosure | user enumeration | mitigate | Messaggio login generico identico; registrazione duplicata -> errore generico "email gia registrata" senza rivelare provider |
| T-03-03 | Elevation of Privilege | route admin | mitigate | `Depends(require_admin)` server-side; 403 per non-admin (AUTH-04) |
| T-03-04 | Spoofing | logout non revoca | mitigate | logout elimina la riga user_session (revoca reale server-side, D-02) |
| T-03-05 | Information Disclosure | leak campi interni | mitigate | risposta UserOut field-by-field, nessun model_validate su ORM |
</threat_model>
